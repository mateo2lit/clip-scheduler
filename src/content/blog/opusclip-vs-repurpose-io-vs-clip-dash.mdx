Search for ways to get more out of your videos and three names keep coming up: OpusClip, Repurpose.io and Clip Dash. They get compared as if they were interchangeable, but each one is built around a different job. Picking the right one depends on which part of the work is eating your time.

*Features and pricing below were checked in September 2026. All three tools change often, so confirm current plans on each site before you buy.*

## The Short Version

| If your problem is... | Look at |
|---|---|
| "I have long videos and no time to find the good moments" | **OpusClip** (AI clip-making) |
| "I post on one platform and want it copied everywhere automatically" | **Repurpose.io** (automated recycling) |
| "I have clips and want to schedule them to every platform, my way" | **Clip Dash** (multi-platform scheduling) |

## OpusClip: Turning Long Videos Into Clips

OpusClip is an AI editor. You give it a long video (a podcast, a VOD, a talk) and it finds highlights, cuts them vertical, adds captions and gives each clip a virality score.

**Strong at:**
- Finding clip-worthy moments in long recordings.
- Auto-captions and vertical reframing.
- Producing lots of clips quickly.

**Keep in mind:**
- Pricing is credit-based, charged per minute of source video you upload, so long streams use credits fast.
- Scheduling is available, but on higher plans; it's a clip maker first.

**Best for:** podcasters and long-form creators who need clips made for them.

## Repurpose.io: Recycling What You Already Posted

Repurpose.io is automation. You set up workflows like "every new TikTok, repost to Reels and Shorts", and it copies your content across platforms, handling resizing and watermark removal.

**Strong at:**
- Set-and-forget cross-posting once workflows are configured.
- Removing watermarks when reposting between platforms.
- Handling many accounts, especially on agency plans.

**Keep in mind:**
- It's built around copying posts, so every platform tends to get the same thing.
- Less suited to writing different captions or timing per platform.

**Best for:** creators with one "home" platform who want the same content mirrored everywhere else.

## Clip Dash: Scheduling Clips Everywhere, Per Platform

Clip Dash is a scheduler built for video creators and streamers. You upload a clip once, or paste a Twitch or Kick clip URL, then publish it to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Bluesky, X and Pinterest with settings and captions per platform.

**Strong at:**
- Twitch and Kick clip import by URL.
- Per-platform captions and settings (TikTok privacy, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels vs Stories, Pinterest boards).
- Multiple accounts per platform, a content calendar and one comment inbox.
- Simple pricing: Creator $9.99/month, Team $19.99/month (up to five teammates).

**Keep in mind:**
- It's a scheduler first. AI clip-making (AI Clips) is included on the Team plan, but if you need heavy AI editing on hours of footage every week, a dedicated editor like OpusClip goes deeper.

**Best for:** streamers and video creators who already have clips (or a clipper) and want them on every platform without logging into each one.

## Using Two Together

These tools overlap less than the comparisons suggest, and plenty of creators pair them:

- **OpusClip + Clip Dash:** OpusClip makes the clips, Clip Dash schedules them to eight platforms with per-platform captions.
- **Your clipper + Clip Dash:** an editor cuts clips by hand and schedules them through your accounts without needing your passwords.
- **Repurpose.io alone** makes sense if you're happy with the same post everywhere and don't need per-platform control.

## How to Decide

Ask where your time goes today:

1. **Finding and cutting moments?** Start with an AI clip-maker, or hire a clipper.
2. **Posting the same thing to many places?** Automation like Repurpose.io is the easiest fix.
3. **Posting different versions to many places, on a schedule?** That's what a multi-platform scheduler like Clip Dash is for.
